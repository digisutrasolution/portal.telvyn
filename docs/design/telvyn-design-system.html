10 · Elevation, radius & density rules

Elevation

L0 flat (page) · L1 --elev-1 (cards, tables, popovers) · L2 --elev-2 (drawers, modals, palette, toasts). Dark mode elevates by surface lightening + hairline, not heavy shadows. Z-scale: 0 / 10 / 40 (drawer) / 50 (modal) / 100 (toast).

Radius & borders

4px badges/chips-sm · 6px controls · 8px containers · 999px only filter chips + status dots. 1px hairlines carry structure; no huge cards, no gradients, no glass. Focus: 2px accent ring, 2px offset, both themes (WCAG 2.2 focus-appearance). Targets ≥24×24px CSS (2.5.8), primary controls ≥32px height.
